Editorial-style travel illustration with a calm, mature and airy aesthetic. The image should feel refined and contemporary, inspired by magazine illustrations rather than children’s books or playful art. Clean composition with a strong sense of space, light and perspective. Limited and harmonious color palette with soft blue, muted green and warm golden tones as dominant colors. Additional colors are allowed only if they are subtle, desaturated and naturally blend into this palette. Smooth shapes, simplified architecture, no exaggerated or toy-like forms. Minimal details, with emphasis on atmosphere rather than precision. Subtle grain or matte texture may be used to add depth and a printed, editorial feel. The overall mood is fresh, calm, thoughtful and inspiring, evoking a sense of real travel, quiet discovery and openness. Avoid cute, playful or cartoonish styles. Avoid children’s book illustration aesthetics. Avoid bright primary colors, neon tones or high contrast. Avoid overly detailed, busy or decorative compositions.
